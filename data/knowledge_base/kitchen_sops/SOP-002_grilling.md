# SOP-002 Grilling Procedure
1. Confirm item and portion against approved recipe.
2. Prepare grill station and clean equipment.
3. Use separate utensils for raw and ready-to-eat ingredients.
4. Season and prepare according to recipe.
5. Follow the separately validated cooking and temperature-control policy.
6. Finish and serve as specified; clean work area.
This sample intentionally does not state food-safety temperatures. Fictional SOP.
