# REF-001 Pasta Types
Spaghetti: long strands; commonly paired with oil-based or tomato sauces. Penne: short tubes; suitable for cream and tomato sauces. Tagliatelle: flat ribbons; often paired with mushroom sauces. Lasagna sheets: broad sheets for layered baked dishes. Follow supplier packaging and approved recipe because cooking times vary. Fictional guide.
