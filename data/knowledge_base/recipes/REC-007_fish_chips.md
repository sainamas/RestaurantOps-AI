# REC-007 Classic Fish and Chips
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: British/Continental | Version: 1.0
Ingredients: white fish fillet, flour, baking powder, sparkling water, fries, lemon and tartar sauce.
1. Pat fish dry and check for bones.
2. Mix flour and baking powder; whisk in chilled sparkling water.
3. Coat fish and fry using the separately approved fryer procedure.
4. Prepare fries using approved procedure. Serve with lemon and tartar sauce.
Allergens: fish, wheat; tartar sauce may contain egg and mustard. Fictional training content.
