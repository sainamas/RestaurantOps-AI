# REC-002 Chicken Alfredo Penne
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: Italian-inspired | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved sample | Servings: 1
## Ingredients
Penne 100 g; chicken breast 120 g; butter 20 g; garlic 5 g; cream 120 ml; Parmesan 30 g; olive oil 1 tsp; salt, pepper and parsley.
## Preparation
1. Cook penne according to package instructions until al dente; reserve pasta water.
2. Season chicken and cook using the restaurant's separately approved poultry procedure. Rest and slice.
3. Melt butter, briefly cook garlic, add cream and simmer gently.
4. Stir in Parmesan. Add penne and chicken, toss and loosen with pasta water if needed.
5. Finish with parsley and serve.
## Allergens
Contains milk. Pasta may contain wheat; verify supplier labels.
