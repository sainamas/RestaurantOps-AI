# REC-004 Classic Vegetable Lasagna
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: Italian | Version: 1.0
Ingredients: lasagna sheets, mixed vegetables, passata, onion, garlic, olive oil, bechamel, mozzarella and Parmesan.
1. Soften onion and garlic in olive oil.
2. Add vegetables and cook until just tender.
3. Add passata and oregano; simmer until thickened.
4. Layer sauce, sheets and bechamel. Repeat and finish with cheese.
5. Bake according to the kitchen's validated oven procedure and rest before portioning.
Allergens: milk; sheets may contain wheat and egg. Fictional training content.
