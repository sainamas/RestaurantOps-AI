# REC-006 Chicken Steak with Pepper Sauce
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: Continental | Version: 1.0
Ingredients: chicken breast, black pepper, butter, cream, stock, garlic, olive oil and salt.
1. Season chicken and cook using the approved poultry procedure.
2. Melt butter, briefly cook garlic, add stock and cream.
3. Simmer gently, add pepper and adjust seasoning.
4. Serve chicken with sauce and mashed potato.
Allergens: milk; check stock label. Fictional training content.
