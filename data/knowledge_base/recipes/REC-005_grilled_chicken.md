# REC-005 Grilled Chicken with Herb Butter
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: Continental | Version: 1.0
Ingredients: chicken breast 180 g, olive oil, butter 20 g, parsley, thyme, lemon zest, salt and pepper.
1. Mix softened butter with herbs and lemon zest; chill.
2. Season chicken and lightly coat with oil.
3. Grill using the restaurant's separately approved cooking and temperature-control procedure.
4. Rest as specified by the approved kitchen procedure. Serve with herb butter and sides.
Allergens: milk. Fictional training content.
