# REC-003 Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: Italian | Version: 1.0
Ingredients: spaghetti 100 g, olive oil 25 ml, garlic 10 g, chilli flakes, parsley and salt.
1. Cook spaghetti until al dente; reserve pasta water.
2. Warm olive oil and gently cook garlic without burning.
3. Add chilli, spaghetti and a splash of pasta water.
4. Toss, add parsley and serve.
