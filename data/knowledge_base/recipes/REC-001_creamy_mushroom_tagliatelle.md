# REC-001 Creamy Mushroom Tagliatelle
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: Italian-inspired | Version: 1.0 | Status: Approved sample | Servings: 1
## Ingredients
Tagliatelle 100 g; mushrooms 80 g; butter 15 g; garlic 5 g; cooking cream 100 ml; Parmesan 20 g; parsley 1 tsp; salt and pepper.
## Preparation
1. Cook tagliatelle according to package instructions until al dente; reserve pasta water.
2. Melt butter, add garlic and mushrooms, and cook until softened.
3. Add cream and simmer gently. Stir in Parmesan.
4. Loosen with pasta water if needed. Toss pasta in sauce, season and garnish with parsley.
## Allergens
Contains milk. Pasta may contain wheat and egg; verify supplier labels.
