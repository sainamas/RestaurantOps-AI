# REC-008 Cream of Mushroom Soup
Category: Recipe | Cuisine: Continental | Version: 1.0
Ingredients: mushrooms 180 g, onion 50 g, butter 20 g, vegetable stock 300 ml, cream 80 ml, garlic, salt, pepper and parsley.
1. Melt butter; soften onion and garlic.
2. Add mushrooms and cook until softened.
3. Add stock and simmer until tender.
4. Blend carefully, return to pan, stir in cream and season.
5. Garnish with parsley.
Allergens: milk; verify stock label. Fictional training content.
